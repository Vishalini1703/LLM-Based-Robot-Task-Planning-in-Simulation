# Consent Checklist

Use this only after institutional approval confirms the required procedure.

- [ ] The participant is an adult student or member of teaching staff in the approved recruitment group.
- [ ] The participant received and could read the participant information.
- [ ] The participant understands that participation is voluntary.
- [ ] The participant understands what data will be collected and how it will be reported.
- [ ] The participant had an opportunity to ask questions.
- [ ] The participant agreed to take part using the institutionally approved consent mechanism.
- [ ] Approval/consent evidence is stored separately from anonymous response data.

Do not put a name, signature, email address, or student number in `responses.csv`.
