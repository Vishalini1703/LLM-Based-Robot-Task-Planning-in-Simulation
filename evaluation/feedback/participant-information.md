# Participant Information

This optional low-risk activity evaluates how understandable a simulated robot-planning prototype appears to adult students and teaching staff. The prototype converts a kitchen instruction into a visible action plan, validates it, and either simulates it or explains why it was rejected.

Participation involves observing the same short demonstration script and completing five 1–5 ratings plus three optional free-text questions. It should take approximately 10 minutes. The activity does not test the participant and has no effect on study or employment.

No names, student numbers, contact details, API keys, health information, or other unnecessary personal information will be collected. Responses use an anonymous code such as `P001`. Participation is voluntary; a participant may stop before submitting. Because submitted responses contain no identity link, an individual response may not be removable after it has been anonymised and combined.

Technical performance and perceived usability are analysed separately. Feedback does not establish robot safety. The study covers simulation only.

Recruitment must not begin until the institution confirms the approved low-risk procedure and any required consent wording.
